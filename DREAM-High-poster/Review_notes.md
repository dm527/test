# DREAM-High poster review

The editable master is `DREAM-High_Poster_48x48.pptx`: one slide, 48 × 48 inches. Text, chart bars, chart labels, and layout shapes are editable. The student portfolio is an embedded image from the source slides. The PDF and PNG are rendered proofs.

## Confirm before printing

- Survey respondent count, cohort/year, timing, and whether denominators vary by item. The source slide gives percentages but no count. Values were retained and rounded to one decimal place; labels were shortened. The results measure student-reported perceptions and intentions.
- Exact funding acknowledgments and grant numbers, plus any required institutional or sponsor logos. These were not invented from the institutional affiliations.
- Permission to publicly display the student portfolio screenshot, including the student's name and portrait.
- Which 2027 developments the grant permits. All are explicitly proposed on the poster.

## Implementation ideas

1. Build a progressive R sequence: modify working code, fill missing steps, debug a script, then design an analysis from a short specification. Offer common core tasks and optional challenges, with mentor checkpoints.
2. Recruit a small alumni advisory group before the course. Pair alumni teachers and mentors with experienced instructors; define responsibilities and provide training.
3. Schedule small-group scientist and patient-advocate conversations around the datasets students analyze, followed by short reflections.
4. Invite an Artist-in-Residence to lead “From Data to Story.” Combine a scientifically reviewed visual narrative with patient-advocate input and an end-of-course exhibition.
5. Compare early and late coding/interpretation tasks using a simple rubric; retain weekly reflections and follow up with alumni.

## Printing and editing

Open the PowerPoint in Microsoft PowerPoint and edit individual objects. Print or export at the original 48 × 48-inch size, 100% scale. Re-export the PDF after edits; check the printer's margins and any bleed requirements. The layout keeps content inside the page, with no bleed-dependent artwork.

Content sources and planning details are also included in the slide's speaker notes. `create_poster.py` regenerates the original draft; rerunning it will overwrite manual edits to that draft file.
